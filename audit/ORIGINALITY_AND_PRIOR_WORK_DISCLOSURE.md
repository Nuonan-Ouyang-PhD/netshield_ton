## Related publication disclosed proactively

The same five authors published:

Nuonan Ouyang, Adrian Shatte, Zhigang Lu, Chao Chen, and Wei Xiang, “SoK: Telemetry-Aware Runtime Assurance for Always-On On-device Intrusion Detection,” *Information Security and Privacy (ACISP 2026), LNCS 16794*, pp. 163–183, Springer, 2026. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-92-3018-1_7

This is disclosed because it is topically related to runtime assurance for constrained IDS, even though it is **not a prior NetShield version**.

## Difference statement

- The ACISP paper is a **Systematization of Knowledge** based on a structured literature corpus.
- It proposes **TQS-IDS as a design template/interface specification** and explicitly states that it is not an implemented system or empirical validation.
- It does **not** contain the NetShield shared-bottleneck formulation, ten-action evidence-scheduling lattice, independent/central/shielded DPP comparison, joint pre-admission mechanism, S01–S12 Raspberry-Pi campaign, 648 device-runs, 58,320 measured probe-windows, frozen session-level statistical analysis, or the empirical results in the ToN manuscript.
- The ToN manuscript therefore uses the SoK only as a related conceptual antecedent and supplies the implemented networking system and real-hardware evidence absent from that work.
