# Cover Letter

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “NetShield: Feasibility Boundaries and
Shared-Bottleneck Evidence Admission for Multi-Probe IoT Edge Intrusion
Detection,” for publication in *IEEE Transactions on Networking*.

The manuscript studies a networking problem at the intersection of edge
security and shared-service control. It derives an exact coverage--richness
feasibility boundary, evaluates coordinated and independent evidence
admission, and measures the timing residuals that remain after byte-feasible
admission on a heterogeneous three-probe Raspberry Pi testbed. The manuscript
also provides a path-free result audit, offline sensitivity analysis, timing
reconstruction, scaling conformance check, and reproducibility instructions.

The work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under
consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree
with its submission. The paper does not claim a new intrusion classifier or
universal classifier/control superiority; its claims are limited to evidence
feasibility, admission, queue behavior, and service timing.

The repository includes the clean manuscript and supplementary material,
machine-readable result tables, validation scripts, and a data/reproduction
guide. Third-party CICIoT2023 files and private hardware raw evidence are not
redistributed in the repository; their access and licensing boundaries are
documented separately.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Nuonan Ouyang, on behalf of all authors
