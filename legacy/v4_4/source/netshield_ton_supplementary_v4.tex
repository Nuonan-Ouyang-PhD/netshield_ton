\section{Scope and Statistical Unit}
This supplement contains statistical and provenance detail omitted from the main manuscript for readability. It does not introduce new experimental data or recompute the frozen empirical results. The formal campaign contains 12 paper-eligible sessions (S01--S12), three capacity regimes, six methods, 216 method--capacity arms, 648 device-runs, and 58,320 measured probe-windows. Each method contributes 108 device-runs and 9,720 measured windows.

The inferential unit is the \emph{session} ($n=12$). Within each session and method, the three capacities and three probes are pooled before paired method differences are formed. The 36 session--capacity cells are descriptive, not independent inferential replicates. For each metric family, all 15 pairwise method contrasts are tested by exact two-sided sign flip over $2^{12}=4096$ assignments, with 10,000-resample session-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals and Holm correction across the 15 contrasts in that family.

\section{Non-Inferiority and Action Consistency}
The prespecified non-inferiority comparison is shielded-DPP (F) versus fixed-summary (B) in timely F1. The frozen result is $F-B=0.000$ with margin $-0.02$, $n=12$ sessions, exact sign-flip procedure, and \texttt{meets\_non\_inferiority=true}. The Holm-adjusted equality-test $p$-value is 1.0; the non-inferiority conclusion follows from the prespecified margin and observed difference rather than from rejecting an equality null.

\section{Unique Shielded-DPP Deadline Miss}
F records one measured deadline violation in 9,720 windows, giving the Table-1 run-macro violation rate 0.000103. The event is S09/high/P3/window~80: decision latency 1315.090898~ms against the 1000-ms deadline, evidence completion 1578.689249~ms, and evidence receipt RTT 1311.904469~ms. Queue bytes are zero, the action is H-FULL, and neighboring RTT values are approximately 66/63~ms. This is retained as evidence that configured byte feasibility is not an unconditional guarantee on stochastic Wi-Fi completion latency.

\section{Primary Table Aggregation Check}
Table-1 timely-F1/FPR/E2E/violation values are unweighted macro means over the 108 device-runs per method. For fixed-full, the run-macro values are timely F1 0.933168 and FPR 0.284536 (reported 0.9332/0.2845). Pooling all window-level confusion counts instead gives micro F1 0.933589 and FPR 0.282292 (reported only as a reference). MaxQ is measured in MiB: C reaches 8,301,290~B $=7.92$~MiB and D reaches 87,382~B $=0.08$~MiB.
